Days of Wine and Roses is a popular song composed by Henry Mancini with lyrics by Johnny Mercer. It was originally written for the 1962 film of the same name, directed by Blake Edwards and starring Jack Lemmon and Lee Remick. The song won the Academy Award for Best Original Song in 1963.

 <!--more-->

The song has since become a jazz standard, covered by many artists including Frank Sinatra, Tony Bennett, Chris Potter (photo) and Bill Evans. Its haunting melody and poignant lyrics capture the bittersweet nature of love and loss.
